Composite Weights

Responsiveness (35%) — Avg response time (44.4%) + Responds quickly survey (33.4%) + Responds helpfully survey (22.2%)
Grading (32%) — Grades reasonable survey (45%) + Feedback valuable survey (35%) + Median grading turnaround (20%)
Environment (25%) — Rewarding environment survey (50%) + Enough instruction survey (30%) + Comfortable asking survey (20%)
Context (8%) — NPS recommendation (60%) + DFW rate (40%)

Response time & grading turnaround use absolute benchmarks. Response: 100 at ≤24h → 0 at ≥48h. Grading: 100 at ≤7 days → 0 at ≥10 days. Survey Likerts, NPS, and DFW rate use cohort min/max. Tier cap: instructors exceeding unacceptable thresholds (response >48h or grading >10 days) are capped at Probation regardless of composite score.
